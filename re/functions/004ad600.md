## Contract

Takes a pointer to a small heap descriptor {HANDLE heap @+0, LPVOID block @+4, dword @+8, dword @+0xc}.
If the block pointer is non-null it HeapFree's it from the descriptor's heap and zeroes +4/+8/+0xc; it then
HeapDestroy's the heap handle at +0 unconditionally. Plain ret (caller cleans: add esp,4 after the call in
0x4571a0), so __cdecl with one argument. Sole caller 0x4571a0 passes the bss descriptor 0x54a178 as the first
step of a teardown loop.
