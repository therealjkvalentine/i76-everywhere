## Contract

Takes an HWND. When the flag at 0x608410 is zero it zeroes a POINT at 0x60842c/0x608430, converts it with
ClientToScreen (so it becomes the screen position of the window's client origin), then calls GetClientRect and
stores right/bottom (the client width/height) into 0x608424/0x608428. When the flag is non-zero it zeroes the
origin and copies the surface width/height globals 0x608400/0x608404 (renderer_surface_width/height, proposed)
into 0x608424/0x608428. So it refreshes a client rectangle (x, y, width, height) for the display window. Both
callers (0x4305e0, 0x431980) are the display-mode setup paths that immediately read 0x608424/0x608428 after it.

## Hypotheses

- hypothesis: 0x608410 (read at 0x433d10, compared with 0 at 0x433d1b) looks like a fullscreen/exclusive-mode flag: when set, origin is 0,0 and size is the surface size; no string or import settles its meaning, so no global claim.
- hypothesis: 0x60842c/0x608430 form a POINT then followed by width/height, i.e. a client RECT-like block in screen coordinates used by the mouse/cursor clamping; not checked outside this function.
