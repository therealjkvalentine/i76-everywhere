## Contract

Takes two float[3] vectors by pointer and modifies both in place: each component is multiplied by 1000.0
(0x4becb4), the y component (offset 4) is set to 0, and the vector is normalised with rsqrt (0x495000) of x*x+z*z,
so both become unit vectors in the XZ plane. It then takes the dot product, clamps it to [-0.9999, 0.9999]
(immediates 0x3f7ff972 / 0xbf7ff972), computes acos via fpatan(sqrt(1-d*d), d), wraps a negative result by
+2*pi, converts to degrees (* 1/pi * 180), and uses the cross product (MSVCRT _isnan guards on all three cross
components, sign of the b.x*a.z - a.x*b.z term) to return 360-angle for the other side. A result of exactly 360
is returned as 0, so the value is an angle in degrees in [0, 360) between the two vectors projected onto XZ.
Callers 0x4a6470 and 0x4a7c80 store the float10 result (fstp) after each call.

## Hypotheses

- hypothesis: The x1000 prescale before normalisation likely avoids rsqrt precision loss on small vectors; callers 0x4a6470/0x4a7c80 may use the angle for heading or aiming, not verified.
- hypothesis: Callers appear to clean the stack later (add esp,8 two instructions after the call at 0x4a6ad7), so __cdecl is likely but Gate K evidence (cleanup immediately after the call) is absent.
