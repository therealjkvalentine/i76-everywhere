## Contract

Applies one hit to the entity behind a world object and, when the entity's flags word
comes out with the "dead" bit set, runs the kill bookkeeping and the kill message.
Three __cdecl arguments (frame: `sub esp,0xc0` at 0x463a80 plus push ebx/ebp/esi/edi;
arg1 read at 0x463a86 `mov ecx,[esp+0xc4]`, arg2 at 0x463a9b `mov ebp,[esp+0xd0]`,
arg3 at 0x463af5 `mov edi,[esp+0xdc]`; the single caller 0x4652b0 pushes (obj, 0, 0)
at 0x4652ec-0x4652f2, calls at 0x4652f9 and cleans with `add esp,0xc` at 0x4652fe).
Returns eax=1 (0x4641bf) after handling, eax=0 (0x4641d2) when the hit is rejected.
