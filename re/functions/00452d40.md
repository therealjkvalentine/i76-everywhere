## Contract

A two-argument setup routine for the network state, called once from WinMain (0x402b30). It loads param 1
(dword, [esp+4] at 0x452d40) and param 2 (word, [esp+8] at 0x452d44), writes 1 to net_game_flag (0x541030),
zeroes 0x541034, 0x609320 and the word 0x541000, writes 0xbf800000 (-1.0f) to 0x4f5aa8 and 0x541014, stores
param 1 into net_dp (0x541024, the ANet dp handle that net_WaitForGroupThenEnumPlayers passes to the dp* calls)
and param 2 into the word 0x541028. It then calls seven no-argument routines and tail-jumps (jmp at 0x452da9)
to an eighth: 0x450f40 (zeroes a 0x32-dword table, registers two callbacks via 0x457270/0x457290),
net_WaitForGroupThenEnumPlayers 0x454090, 0x4566a0 (zeroes two tables), 0x455de0 (recreates the NVCL vcf
directory), 0x453bc0 / 0x451ac0 / 0x455820 / 0x455620 (each builds "courier" font descriptors via 0x49b0e0 and
resets their own state words).
