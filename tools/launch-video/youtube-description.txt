Interstate '76 Everywhere: the 1997 game, fixed for 2026.

Side by side: the original game against the i76-everywhere patch kit. Longer draw distance (600 m to 2300 m), a feathered far edge,
terrain that morphs instead of popping, a working rear mirror, a smooth 120 fps picture on the original 41.667 ms physics tick,
force feedback for wheels, co-op and AI cars on LAN, and 100+ other fixes.

Get it: https://github.com/therealjkvalentine/i76-everywhere
