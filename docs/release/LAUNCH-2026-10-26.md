### 2.1 Forum / Discord post (GOG Interstate forum as a new thread; UltraFunk Discord without the title)

**Title:** Interstate '76 at 120 fps with the 1997 physics, widescreen, 2.3 km draw distance: a free patch kit for the GOG version (public beta)

> Hi all. I've spent the last few months fixing Interstate '76 around its own 1997 code, and the patch kit is ready for
> other people to try. It goes over your GOG install (nothing of the game is in it), keeps every original file, and has
> an uninstaller that puts the folder back.
>
> - 120 fps, with the car still simulated 24 times a second like on a 1997 Pentium, so the handling, the jumps and the AI
>   are the ones you remember (the mission 5 canyon jump lands on the 1997 numbers)
> - your monitor's real shape (16:9, 21:9): you see more of the world, nothing stretched
> - draw distance 600 m -> 1.8 km, or 2.3 km fading into the sky in the preview launcher, with hills that blend in instead
>   of popping
> - a rear-view mirror that shows the road, menus that work with a modern mouse, music that keeps playing in missions
> - the Windows 11 start-up crash fixed (dgVoodoo 2.87.5 + a winmm fix)
> - keyboard, gamepad, wheel and flight-stick layouts; your own bindings are kept if you have them; UPDATE.bat for new versions
>
> Also in the repo, experimental: LAN games with computer cars filling the empty seats, a two-player co-op campaign, Nitro
> "Arena Drop-In + AI", wheel force feedback with a live tuning page, head tracking with a webcam, a Mac (Wine) build and a
> Steam Deck kit.
>
> Trailer and download: https://github.com/therealjkvalentine/i76-everywhere
> It's a public beta: bug reports with the game folder's mciproxy.log are what I need most, and the GitHub issues have a
> "waiting for QA" list anyone with the hardware can help with. Hats off to vahokif's I26, a reimplementation that takes
> the other road and is well worth a look.
