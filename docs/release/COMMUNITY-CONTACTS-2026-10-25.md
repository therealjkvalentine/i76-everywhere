# Community contacts for the v0.7 announcement (2026-10-25)

Who demonstrably cares about Interstate '76, and the **public** route to reach each of them. Built from
[COMMUNITY-RESOURCES.md](../COMMUNITY-RESOURCES.md), [COMMUNITY-SITEMAP.md](../COMMUNITY-SITEMAP.md),
[research/content-2026-10-24/](../research/content-2026-10-24/), [PRIOR-ART-I26-2026-10-21.md](../PRIOR-ART-I26-2026-10-21.md),
and a fresh sweep on 2026-10-25: YouTube search (10 + 9 queries, 40 / 30 results each, metadata read from the public watch
and channel-about pages), the GOG Interstate forum index, GitHub profiles (`gh api users/<name>`), and Discord's public
invite lookup. **Nothing was sent and nothing was posted.** The announcement texts are in
[ANNOUNCEMENT-v0.7.md](ANNOUNCEMENT-v0.7.md).

**Rules this list keeps.** Only routes the person or site publishes: an email shown on their own site or GitHub profile, a
site contact page, a GitHub issue tracker, a public Discord invite, a forum thread they started, a YouTube channel's About
links. No email was guessed and nothing private was scraped. YouTube business emails sit behind YouTube's signed-in "View
email address" button: they were **not** read; open the channel's About page while signed in if one is wanted.

**Status column.**
- **verified public**: the route was read on 2026-10-25 at the URL given (or, for emails, on the page named).
- **listed, not re-verified**: the route is published but could not be read today (site refused the client); the URL is
  where it is published.
- **no public route found**: say so rather than guess.

Counts at the end.
