Interstate '76 on the Deck: the gamepad layout

The Deck uses exactly the layout of an Xbox pad on Windows and Mac, so there is one set of controls to learn. The only Deck extras are the two trackpads: a quick-select menu on the left, the menu pointer on the right. The back grips do nothing.

Watch for these

LB is two buttons.

A quick tap targets the car in front. Holding it turns on the shift layer: while it is down, the triggers, A, B, X, Y and the D-pad do the jobs marked LB + above.

A: tap or hold.

A tap is OK in menus and one shot in the car. Holding it for about half a second fires nitrous for as long as you hold.

Looking back changes RT and R3.

Hold the right stick back to look behind you; while you do, RT fires the rear gun and clicking the right stick drops mines.

The left stick click has two jobs.

A plain click is nitrous. Pulled back and clicked, it puts the car in reverse.

The right stick depends on the view.

Inside the car it glances and springs back. Outside it swings the camera around the car, and the camera stays where you leave it.

The layout is the shared pad layer (i76-remap.ahk, controls/input.map), the same files Windows and Mac use; the Windows/Mac quick-reference sheet shows the same buttons. On the Deck it is the Steam Input layout "Interstate 76 - gamepad (v10)", which the launcher pairs with the pad layer.
