# AI car names: "<sci-fi name> (AI)", replaced on drop-in (2026-10-25)

Owner, 2026-10-25, playing co-op with AI cars: *"AI cars should have (AI) after and AI names like HAL9000, MOTHER, etc. from
sci-fi (I, Robot) - names only if they get updated/replaced on drop-in."* Issue #91.

**Result:** proxy commit `8a305af` (`music-fix/aimelee.c`, `music-fix/nitro_ai.c`), `STRLKUP.DLL` **d8adba835ed64880ab0f20c9750d87fb**
(`tools/build-proxy.ps1 -Ref 8a305af`, built twice byte-identical: lab `builds\ai-names-20261025\prod-8a305af-r1|r2`). On the laptop
LAN pair both peers list the same 7 "<NAME> (AI)" labels (n 2), and a human who drops in takes one AI's seat: that AI's label leaves
both lists and the human's own row is shown, with no stale AI label on either side (n 2). 0 CRASH lines in 8 boots.

## How the name is made (no new packet)

- Each AI car already has an **AI number k**: its place among the mission's AI cars in label-table order (`aim_ordinal`, counted
  once per load). It is the same on every machine because every machine loads the same arena file and the same injected AI pool.
  It already set the row id (0x7E00 + k) and the old AUTO MELEE name.
- Label = `AI_NAMES[(k + FNV-1a(mission file name, lower case)) % 29] + " (AI)"`. Every peer computes it on its own, so PC and Mac
  peers on the same proxy md5 agree without anything new on the wire. The mission hash only changes the cast from arena to arena;
  within a session it is fixed.
- **Width:** the player-table name is 24 bytes (`0x454620` / `0x454680` copy 0x18), and the Tab scoreboard (`0x451f10`) prints it
  with `%-15s` under the 15-character "Player Name" header. So every label is at most 15 characters, i.e. names of up to 10 plus
  " (AI)". **Dropped, too long:** DEEP THOUGHT, NEUROMANCER. The 29 kept: HAL 9000, MOTHER, SKYNET, JOSHUA, GLaDOS, KITT, VIKI,
  SONNY, MARVIN, GORT, ROBBY, BISHOP, ASH, TARS, CASE, HOLLY, SHODAN, EDI, CORTANA, COLOSSUS, PROTEUS, ED-209, DAVID, AVA, SAMANTHA,
  WINTERMUTE, DURANDAL, MULTIVAC, ROY.
- **Where it shows:** the AI rows of the player table (the Tab scoreboard; the stock "<X> was killed by <AI>" line and the HUD name
  read them by owner id) and the proxy's own "<AI> was destroyed by <player>" line (`aim_credit`). The kill lines use the same
  function as the rows but no kill happened in these runs: they are not measured here.
- **Switch:** `I76_AI_NAMES` (default 1 wherever the AI cars have rows; `0` = the stock names: Gold's AUTO MELEE list from the shell,
  Nitro's seven-name list, with no suffix). Nitro's ARENA+ AI rows (`nai_name`) use the same table, seeded by its pool's mission
  name. **Nitro is not tested.**

## Drop-in: why no stale label can remain

There is no rename step to get wrong. A human who joins does not inherit an AI's row: the host's AI fill (mpfill.c) parks one AI car
(the farthest from every human) and gives its row back (`aim_rows_tick`); a joiner drops that row once the host's 'AT' marks the
car parked (byte 3, 0x40). The human gets his own row from the stock ANet player data, with his broadcast name. When he leaves, the
fill brings an AI back, under its own AI number and therefore the same label as before.

## Test (laptop, Gold pair)

Harness: lab `tools\ai-names\an.py` on the lanfix / coop-match harness (copies `game-mpo-c` host and `game-mpo-d` joiner, the laptop's
own desktop, IPXWrapper G / H set to log_level 4 for the job and restored, `an-job.ps1` holding the bench lock). Every boot
`I76_SKIP_MOVIES=1`, `I76_SKIP_CUTSCENES=1`, `I76_FFB_OFF=1`; preset OWNER-2300-ALL; arena Suburbia (`m04.msn`, the stock arena

11865599). The measurement reads the player table (`0x541070`, 16 x 0x48: id, name, flags), the table the scoreboard prints, on

- **Interval:** the host lists the joiner and the still-playing AI together for up to 1.5 s (0.0-0.25 s and 0.0-1.5 s). That is the
  fill waiting 1.5 s for the human count to settle before it parks a car, and in that time the AI car is still in play. The
  joiner never shows the parked AI: its AI rows come with the first 'AT' flags (~4 s), and by then the car is marked parked.
- **Names:** the lab copies have no player name set, so both humans read "Unnamed" (the stock default). The human row is told apart
  by its ANet id (6 = the joiner), not by its text.
- **Drop-out (extra):** after the joiner's process was killed the host still listed him 25 s later (the stock ANet drop timeout),
  so the AI coming back was not seen in the harness window. Not measured.
- 0 CRASH lines in 8 boots (4 runs x host + joiner).
