# AI melee M1: computer opponents in a network melee arena (2026-10-19)

Design: [AI-MELEE-AND-LATE-JOIN-2026-10-19.md](AI-MELEE-AND-LATE-JOIN-2026-10-19.md), option (b). The AI cars are
objects in the arena file. The host runs them, and the co-op sync (`I76_COOP_AI`) shows them to everyone. This record
says what was built, which defaults were chosen while the owner was away, and what was measured on two lab copies on
the PC.

**Status.** The host tick, the kill credit, the waves and the automatic respawn are built and measured on the PC
(section 3). Two things are not covered. **A joiner's kill of an AI car was not achieved:** the scripted joiner could not aim
well enough in 5 sessions. Its credit path is the host's own path with another player id, but that is unmeasured.
Nothing was run across two machines. Commits: i76-everywhere `e1f35f0` and `0789dc3` (code) and the commit carrying this record; the lab commit with
`i76-uncap-lab\tools\aimelee\`. Pack:
`i76-uncap-lab\builds\mp-pack-aimelee-20261019\` (deployed nowhere). Its proxy is md5 8e262bcf: main `e9d3e0b`, which has
the AI melee code of `0789dc3` plus later commits from other lanes. That build was not run in game by this lane; the
runs used a4 / a5 (section 3).

## 1. What was built

| piece | where | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| arena files | `tools/aimelee-mission.py` | A stock arena (`miss16\M07.MSN`, Hope Springs) plus N AI cars, written to `ADDON\ai_hs2.lvl`, `ai_hs4.lvl` and `ai_hs6.lvl` (lower case). Each car is an ODEF class-1 car record on team 7, labelled with a multiplayer car variant that `I76.ZFS` holds (67 of them: the `i76car.def` stems x variants 1..3). The cars stand on road centre lines, at least 80 m from every spawn and regen point and 30 m from scenery, spread by farthest-point sampling. The pick is the stock AUTO MELEE rule (a random multiplayer car) with a fixed seed, so every build of a file is byte-identical. The title is set in the WRLD record. Run by `coop-install.py`, and through it by `mp-pack.py` |
| host tick | `music-fix/aimelee.c`, at `0x40a5d0` | This site is the `call 0x401310` "single-player melee?" test inside `ai_FrameTick`. In a network game the test is 0, so the stock melee tick `0x40a130` never runs, and the host's death cannot end the game through it. When the host has loaded an `ai_*.lvl`, it gives every live AI car `ai_SetBehaviour(car, target, 0 = attack)` each frame, as `0x40a130` does for the world root. The target is the nearest live human of the 16 player slots. The car keeps that target until it dies or another human is 20 % nearer. Once per car the tick sets skill 3 (`0x40bd90` and `ai+0xa818`, as `0x40a270`), and it gives the host's car attacker cap 8 (`0x40c0a0`, as the stock melee) |
| kill credit | `aimelee.c`, at `0x46419d` | This is the `call 0x401310` near the end of `entity_DamageAndKill`, reached once per kill with edi = victim and ebp = damage record. On the host, an AI victim whose attacker maps to a player id (`0x4551f0`) is queued. The pump sends it as 'AK' {seq, killer id, victim label index, score}, 3 copies, deduplicated by sequence number. The score is the stock `0x454ff0` formula: victim mass x killer 1/mass x 1000. The killer's own machine adds 1 kill and the score to its own slot through `0x454ea0`, which also runs the MAX SCORE test. Its 'ST' packets then carry the numbers to everyone (stock). Every machine prints "<AI name> was destroyed by <player>" on the message line (`0x453df0`) |
| AI names | `aimelee.c` | The k-th AI car of an arena takes name (5 + 13 k) mod 53 from the shell's AUTO MELEE list (`i76shell` `0x1004b438`: "Torque Daisy", ...). The name shows only in the kill message |
| waves | `aimelee.c` + the co-op chain | When every AI car has been dead for 8 s, the host reloads the same arena in the same session through the co-op chain's 'AM' path (kind 0, state 7). The reload resets the player table (measured: kills, deaths and score read 0 after it). Each machine therefore saves its own slot at the reload and writes it back through `0x454ea0` once its car is in the table again |
| respawn | `aimelee.c` | In an `ai_*.lvl`, a dead human (host or joiner) asks for a respawn (`0x451570(1)`, the co-op scenes' automatic respawn) |
| hooks in `strlkproxy.c` | 4 lines | `#include "aimelee.c"` after the co-op section, `apply_aimelee()` in DllMain after `apply_coop_ai`, and one call in `coop_dpReceive` |

Switches (rows in `music-fix/README.md`):

| switch | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `I76_AIMELEE` | on, when `I76_COOP_AI=1` (it uses that switch's `dpReceive` hook and 'AT' sync) | acts only when the loaded arena is `ai_*.lvl`; `=0` turns everything off (the AI cars then sit idle) |
| `I76_AIMELEE_SKILL` | 3 | 1..5, the stock skill scale |
| `I76_AIMELEE_CAP` | 8 | attacker cap on the host's car (only the world root has an "on deck" limit) |
| `I76_AIMELEE_TEAMS` | 0 | 1 = attack only humans whose team (object flags bits 16..18) differs from the AI's |
| `I76_AIMELEE_WAVE` / `_WAVE_S` | 1 / 8 s | waves on, and how long every AI must be dead first; a wave needs `I76_COOP_CHAIN=1` (PLAY-LAN.bat sets it) |
| `I76_AIMELEE_RESPAWN` | 1 | automatic respawn of dead humans in an AI arena |
| `I76_AIMELEE_TEST_HP` | off | LAB ONLY: the host cuts each AI car's armour and chassis to n % when the arena loads, so a test kill takes seconds |

## 2. Defaults chosen while the owner was away (each can be overruled)

1. **The AI count comes from the arena file.** Each count is its own list entry, name first:
   "Hope Springs (2 AI)", "Hope Springs (4 AI)", "Hope Springs (6 AI)". The files are `ADDON\ai_hs2.lvl` / `ai_hs4.lvl`
   / `ai_hs6.lvl`. Joiners need no setup.
2. **The AI are hostile to every human.** They are team 7 ("Men In Black"), and the tick ignores teams unless
   `I76_AIMELEE_TEAMS=1`. With that switch on, a human who picks Men In Black would be left alone.
3. **Waves:** when the last AI dies, the arena reloads in the same session. **Scores are kept.** The reload itself
   zeroes the table (measured), and the proxy writes each player's own numbers back.
4. **AI names** come from the stock 53-name list, but only in the kill message. The scoreboard and the roster list
   the player table only. Fake rows there were judged too risky (design section 1).
5. **AI cars** are the stock AUTO MELEE random pick from the multiplayer list (ZFS variants only), at the default
   skill 3.
6. **No UI change.**
7. **Humans score for AI kills** through 'AK'. A human killed by an AI loses 500 (floored at 0) and gains a death.
   That is stock: `0x455130` treats a killer with no player id like a suicide. The owner may want this changed.
8. **The host's death does not end the game,** and every human respawns by itself after about 5 s.
9. **Late join stays stock** (accepted at any time). The joiner gets the AI through 'AT', as in co-op.

Observations:
- **The AI keep their distance.** Most AI cars hold at 150-190 m from their target and fire from there, many with
  turrets. Some cars circle about 10 m around one spot: in run 2, `vpjrabt2` circled at 2440,50781 for over two
  minutes, 180 m from the joiner, without hitting it. That is the AI's own tactic choice, the same SetBehaviour call
  that stock melee makes each frame. A circling car can hold up a wave for as long as nobody kills it. The forward
  guns of a scripted test car rarely hit at that range; kills came when an AI car came within about 100 m.
- **Joiners see AI health as 100 %.** The 'AT' record carries the damage levels, but the joiner's health fraction of
  a mirrored car stays 100 %. Only the destroyed flag arrives. This is cosmetic (smoke and fire on damaged AI may not
  show for joiners) and is not checked further.
- Humans also shoot each other, as in stock melee: "target nearest" picks the other human too. In run 3 the joiner
  killed the host 3 times.
- After a wave the k-th car has the same name as before (the names follow the car's place in the file).

## 4. What the owner must decide

1. The death penalty: should being killed by an AI cost 500 like a suicide (stock), or nothing?
2. Which arenas besides Hope Springs (Vigilantes' Paradise and Tombstone are the other daylight-and-hills picks), and
   which counts.
3. AI skill (3 now) and whether the 150-190 m stand-off feels right in play. A closer, rammier mix may need skill 4-5
   or a different behaviour id.
4. Waves (now) or a reserve pool (continuous respawn, design section 1). Also whether a circling AI should be
   timed out so a wave cannot stall.
5. AI names in the scoreboard, which needs an overlay: worth it?

## 5. What is still missing

- **Across two machines:** nothing here was run between machines. The pack is built, not deployed.
- **Teams (M3):** the tick has the other-team rule behind `I76_AIMELEE_TEAMS=1`, which was not run. For AI on a
  human team, `aimelee-mission.py` needs a team per car (now always 7). The scoreboard's team sums do not include AI
  (no player-table row). "3 humans vs 3 AI" is not tested.
- **Host migration:** if the host leaves, ANet makes a joiner the host. Its proxy then runs the tick on mirrored
  copies whose state is untested.
- **A circling or stuck AI holds up the wave:** no time-out (decision 4).
- **A joiner's kill of an AI** is not measured (section 3). Next step: a person at the joiner's keyboard, or one LAN night.
- **Ram kills** give no credit: a death with no attacker record is logged, not scored (stock does the same for humans).
- **AI kills of humans** give no AI score (there is no AI row).
- **Mac / Deck:** the files are plain `.lvl` + the proxy, so the Macs get them through the pack (`apply-pack-delta.sh`)
  like the co-op files. The Deck has its own pack (not updated).
