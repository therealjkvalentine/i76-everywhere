# FFB: the owner's first real-wheel drive, and what changed (2026-10-24)

Wheel: Thrustmaster T300. Launcher: `rc-twin-20261024\PLAY-WITH-FFB.bat` (`PLAY-i76.ps1 -Ffb` ->
`Interstate 76\_ffb\ffb-interposer.ps1`), preset OWNER-2300-ALL (24 ticks/s, 41.667 ms).

## 1. What the owner reported

1. "PLAY-WITH-FFB felt good! The terrain was a really great start. The under/oversteer jerk was pronounced."
2. "A little smoother/weaker on pavement (maybe 50 %) - reward the pavement, punish offroad; keep current offroad."
3. "I ran FFB-CALIBRATE and did the steady turns, but there was sliding in the dirt and it said no loss-of-control
   samples, which was weird."
4. "I did not feel any FFB for the engine, ignition or weapons."

## 2. Pavement at 50 % (item 2)

- Surface = `entity+0x45c` (one value per car; the engine has no per-wheel surface), an index into the level's WRLD
  surface table `0x644220` (8 x 20 B: +0 grip, +4 rolling resistance, +8 bump, +0x10 damage rate; <re-archive>
  data/notes/terrain-text-sound.md). Tarmac = rolling resistance <= 0.015, read from the running level (stock:
  types 2 and 6); the index set {2, 6} is the fallback (`Tel-SurfacePaved`).
- `FfbMixer.ps1`: road texture (roughness and bump terms) and the LFE road bed x `PavementScale` (0.50) on tarmac,
  x `OffroadScale` (1.00) elsewhere, through a first-order crossfade `SurfaceFadeMs` (450 ms) so the one-tick flip at
  a road edge is not a step. Panel: `surf N paved|offroad xF`. All three are tune values.
- Live (run r2, T05): the surface went 2 -> 1 (sand) and back; the factor read 50 -> 53 -> 58 -> 61 ... 91 -> 100 %
  per log row (no step), and 96 -> 93 % going back onto tarmac.

## 3. The under/oversteer jerk (item 1) - a wrong reference, not a gain

The slip channels compare the yaw rate with the yaw the steering should give. That reference was the fitted pair
(a, lock) from `_ffb\ffb-calib.json` - in the RC copy the August calibration, a = 31.0 m/s^2. The owner's own
calibration drive today (his car) fits **a = 37.5**: the engine's lateral limit is `7.84 x the grip of tyres 0, 1,
4, 5` (physics_ComputeSteerYawRate 0x43ce10, <re-archive> subsystems/physics.md), car- and damage-dependent, and his tyres
carry the size-factor grip bonus. Against a = 31 his car rotated faster than "commanded" in every hard turn:
**781 of 957 samples (82 %) of his steady-turn drive read as oversteer** (offline, his `ffb-calib-samples.csv`),
i.e. a counter-steer push plus the grip lightening, on and off - the jerk.

Fix: `Tel-Slip` now uses the engine's law read live - `w = steer x s x K` (K = entity+0x110 = 1/(2 wb)), capped at
`steer x lim / s` (lim = 7.84 x tyre grip; the arcade-flag variant too). Live read on two exes: K 0.1880 and lim
31.4 (stab-gold, fresh tyres 4 x 1.0), K 0.1881 / lim 31.36 (r1, the RC twin). His drive implies K 0.19 / lim 37.5.
Test 18 in `ffb-mixer-test.ps1`: a drive on the law reads zero slip in both regimes; his full-lock turn (11.58 m/s,
2.198 rad/s) is flagged by the old fallback and clean on the law. The fitted pair stays as the fallback for logs.

Second, smaller cause found live (r1): the slide term (yaw rate minus the rate the velocity turns) saturated the
oversteer channel at 4-7 m/s with neither engine slide flag set - tick-alignment noise. The slide now counts only
while the engine says the car slides (flags 0x2000 drift band or 0x2 full slide); on the grip path the engine snaps
the velocity to the heading, so there is no slide to report.

The oversteer gain (3600) is unchanged: with both false sources gone, what remains is real slides and spins. Whether
that is now right is the owner's call (checklist).

## 4. "No loss-of-control samples" while sliding (item 3)

Three causes (`ffb-calibrate.ps1` header):
1. **Wrong quantity.** The count was |yaw - model| > 0.5 rad/s. I'76's dirt slide is the drift band: |w s| > L sets
   flag 0x2000, the velocity is KEPT instead of snapped to the heading, and the body still yaws at the FULL commanded
   rate (0x43a5d0 stores w unscaled). A yaw-vs-steering test cannot see it by construction. His drive confirms: max
   deviation 0.177 rad/s over 957 samples, so 0 above 0.5, while he was sliding.
2. **Nothing recorded to see it with**: the samples CSV kept t, speed, steer, yaw only.
3. **A different threshold from the mixer** (0.5 vs 0.08).
4. (Tick rate, checked as asked) the poll was ~31 ms under a 41.7 ms tick: about one repeated sample per tick. That
   does not hide a slide but did make the steadiness derivatives 0-then-spike; they are tick-aware now.

Fix: the samples file keeps slide, the mixer's over/under, the flags, the surface and K / lim per sample; the report
prints "mixer reacts" (Tel-Slip with the gated slide), "strongly", "sliding", the engine's drift/slide flag counts,
the live steering law, and the old yaw-only count labelled as such. Old 4-column logs still load (verified on his
file: mixer reacts 94, strongly 0, slide "not recorded", yaw-only 0). `ffb-calib-test.ps1` 15/15.

Also found: he ran the REPO's `tools\ffb\FFB-CALIBRATE.bat`, which writes `tools\ffb\ffb-calib.json`; the RC
interposer reads `Interstate 76\_ffb\ffb-calib.json`, so his calibration never reached the game. `_ffb` now has its
own `FFB-CALIBRATE.bat`, and his measured file (a 37.5, lock 0.72, sign +1) was copied into the twin's and the
stage's `_ffb` (the August file is kept in `_ffb\_pre-20261024-ffb-lane`). With the live law it only matters as the
fallback and for the sign.

## 5. Engine, ignition, weapons (item 4) - built

Why nothing was felt: no engine or ignition channel existed on the wheel; weapons read the FFB effect block
`0x4f2328` slots, which the exe writes only while its own FFB device is present (`0x52bbd0`), and nothing reported
whether it was. Everything new reads engine state that exists whenever a car does (`Tel-Extras`, Telemetry.ps1):

| channel | source | shape (stock .frc it follows) | default |
|---|---|---|---|
| weapon, class 2 gun / cannon | the player's weapon instances' ammo (+0x20) dropping; class / damage from weapdef +0x5c / +0x60 | `recoil`: +100 % push, -95 % pull, decay (CANNON1..4 UserDefined curves) | WeaponGain 3400 x sqrt(dmg/20) clamped 0.6..1.6, 120 ms + 3 ms/dmg |
| weapon, class 3 mortar | same | recoil x 1.6, 330 ms | MortarScale |
| weapon, class 4 rocket | same | `buzz` 6 Hz 240 ms (MISSILE2/5: one Cosine cycle) | RocketScale 1.3 |
| weapon, class 5 flamer | same, continuous | 12 Hz bed while ammo falls | FlameGain 500 |
| weapon, class 6 dropper | same | short jolt | DropGain 1300 |
| weapon cycle | weapon record +4 (selected row) changing | `ramp` +100 -> -100 % in 70 ms (WPNCYCLE RampDown) | CycleGain 1500 |
| hit | armour + chassis per side (entity +0x138 / +0x148) falling | left / right: signed jolt; front / back: 11 Hz buzz; (40 + 60 x dmg/90) % like the stock ordnance formula | HitGain 4200, HitSideSign +1 (unverified) |
| landing | flag 0x4 falling edge, sized by the fastest descent | 9 Hz buzz 200 ms | LandGain 3000 |
| ignition | flag 0x8 (starting) rising edge; a running edge without one gets a shorter crank | `crank` (ENGSTART: SquareLow 13 Hz +/-58 % 79 ms, then SquareHigh +49/-31 % under 34/10/56 % envelope) | IgnitionGain 2600, 1300 ms |
| engine | flag 0x1 running, rpm from the engine component (+0x1c) | 8 -> 14 Hz oscillator, fades to 0 by 18 m/s (the stock DLL's (20 - mph/2) % shape) | EngineGain 420 |
| shift | gear (engine +0x08) 2..4 changing | existing one firm hit | unchanged |

Dedupe rules: an ammo-path shot suppresses the FFB-block slot kick (that path stays the fallback when no weapon
record is found); a collision's damage and its jolt are one kick; a landing replaces the jolt it causes. The FFB
block still feeds the LFE blast level. Each channel has panel solo / mute (`-Only` / `-Mute`), and a tune saved
with `[s]` now persists (`ffb-tune-owner.json`, only the changed values, loaded by default - before this, PLAY-i76
started the interposer without `-Tune`, so a saved tune was never read).

The .frc parameter block is decoded (`parse-frc.py`, table in the script): kind, type id, direction, duration,
polar flag, envelope percentages (they sum to 100 in all 28 files), frequency, max/min %, UserDefined samples at
10 ms, Sequence / Superimpose children. The SideWinder edition (archive.org compendium, `<archive>\community-
20261024\dl\compendium-disk1\FRC`) decodes to the same effects; only the four cannon curves are re-sampled (1-4 %).

Blown tyre: left as is (not tested by the owner). The flat flags are read by the proxy telemetry, not yet here.

## 7. The owner's next wheel session (checklist)

Launch `PLAY-WITH-FFB.bat` as before. The panel's 5th line now shows `rpm / slide / gear / surf / ENG / DRIFT /
SLIDE / ammo`.
1. Panel says `ammo` (weapon record found) and the `surf` field reads `paved x0.50` on tarmac, `offroad x1.00` off it.
2. Pavement vs off-road: tarmac should feel about half as busy; crossing the road edge should glide, not step.
   Too weak / strong: `PavementScale` in `_ffb\ffb-tune-owner.json` (e.g. `{ "PavementScale": 0.4 }`).
3. Steady turns on tarmac: the on/off under/oversteer jerk should be gone. A real slide in the dirt (panel `DRIFT`)
   should go light then push toward the catch. Still too much: `OversteerGain` (3600) / `UndersteerDrop` (0.75).
4. Fire each weapon you carry (gun, cannon, rocket, mortar, flamer, mines): one recoil per shot, rockets a shove,
   the flamer a buzz while held; cycling weapons a short click. (Not verified live - the first real test.)
5. Get hit on the left, then on the right: the rim should kick AWAY from the hit side. If it kicks toward it, set
   `"HitSideSign": -1`.
6. Jump something: one thump on landing.
7. Engine: a light idle rumble parked, fading out by ~40 mph. Turn the engine off and on (ignition key): a crank.
   Too much / little: `EngineGain` (420), `IgnitionGain` (2600).
8. Optional: `Interstate 76\_ffb\FFB-CALIBRATE.bat` (writes into `_ffb` now). Its report shows the slide and the
   engine drift / slide flag counts next to the old yaw-only one.
9. `[s]` saves what you changed with `+/-` into `ffb-tune-owner.json`; it loads next time.
