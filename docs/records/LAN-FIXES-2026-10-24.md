## 2. Co-op scaling in scenarios and scenes

**Cause:** the scenarios (`auto*.lvl`) had no parked copies (coop-install built them without `--copies`), the host's scaling tick
ran only in `coopNN.lvl`, and a copy comes in only once its original "engages" a human by behaviour, which many enemies never do
(Oil Well Well's go for the wells: 1 original of 8 in 90 s).
**Fix:** `tools/coop-install.py` builds the scenarios with 3 copies per enemy (not Race No Shoot, auto05: racers); the tick runs in
scenarios (`aimelee.c`); in scenarios an enemy given any target counts as engaged; and in both kinds an enemy counts as engaged
once a live human is within `I76_COOP_SCALE_NEAR` (500 m). The six new files (auto01-04, 06, 07; md5 in docs/RC-20261024.md s.11)
are byte-identical to `coop-install.py`'s output; every machine needs them (the join's CRC check).

The copies come in where the fight is, so a test that never drives brings few; a played scene brings more. Copies are

## 3. Joiners spawn near the host

**Cause:** the owner's own car is built by `0x451180(own id, 0x451030() = a random free spawn point)` (sites 0x403ab8, 0x403cd5,
0x456f17), and in a co-op scene / scenario the spawn points sit beside the mission's start.
**Fix (`I76_COOP_NEAR_HOST`, with `I76_COOP_CHAIN`):** on a joiner whose host car is alive, the spawn entry is replaced for that
one call by a pose on the host's trail (positions every 250 ms) 20-40 m behind it, else 25-35 m behind / 25 m beside, 10 m clear of
every car or wreck, ground + 1.5 m (0x493550), facing the host. A join builds the car before the host id is known, so a car built
at the join and > 60 m from the host is moved once within 10 s.

## 4. CANCEL in the multiplayer menu

**Cause** (sampler, 4 of 4): leaving the IPX form destroys the ANet session; dpDestroy unloads WIPX, which unloads IPXWrapper.
`ipxwrapper.dll`'s DLL_PROCESS_DETACH waits up to 3 s for its router thread under the loader lock (the thread cannot exit there,
so the wait always runs out), then WSACleanup.
**Fix (`I76_IPX_PIN`):** a watch thread pins ipxwrapper.dll once it is loaded.

| click to the menu (includes ~0.5-0.7 s of the lab click itself) | host form | join list | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| before (n1) | 5084, 5020 ms | 4929, 5019 ms | 4 |
| after (n3) | 1390, 1993 ms | 1997, 2067 ms | 4 |
| after (n8, ALL preset) | 2103, 1979 ms | 1880, 1992 ms | 4 |

What is left (~1 s) is the sockets' closesocket in mswsock under IPXWrapper.

## 5. Host restart freeze

Measured: host death -> "host lost" -> both reload -> host playable **1.7-2.0 s** after the reload line, joiner 2.0-2.3 s (fam,
n3, n8, n10; coop01 and auto04; n 18 reloads). With a 10 s slow host (`--slowhost 10`): playable 1.4-2.0 s after it resumes
(n 6). The minute-long freeze did not reproduce PC-to-PC. Two things were found and fixed on that path:
- the host's join handshake after a chain reload waits for 5 s without any 'XX' from anyone (0x452f20), and nothing ever sends
  the host an 'SP'; `I76_COOP_HOST_WAIT_MS` (3000) ages it on the machine that was host at the reload (fires: "host handshake
  after the reload 3016 ms old");
- under the ALL preset the joiner's ai_FrameTick hook was never installed (section 8), so joiners ran the mission script and AI
  themselves - a joiner whose script runs its own version of the scene can hold or desync the reload.
**Open:** if the owner sees it again, the host's `mciproxy.log` "coop-chain:" lines around the reload say which wait it was.

## 6. Host-death crash (owner, Mac host; Andrew's joiner)

**Cause:** the crash lines (host i76.exe+0x684BD read 0x30; joiner +0xB902 and +0x9D8D0 read 0x40) and the lab's (fam after a
migration: host + joiner +0x94DB5 read 0x18; n5 with a host respawn request: host +0x9D669 read 0x40, stack through the FSM
dispatcher 0x41311D / ai_FrameTick 0x40A649) are all **mission-script actions** (dispatcher 0x412ce0) on an entity that 0x45f0f0
resolved to NULL: 0x468440 world position, 0x40b8e0 within-distance, 0x49d5f0 / 0x49d740 script camera. The entity is the host's
car = the mission's player object, removed when the host **respawned** from the death view (0x451180 removes the old car), or the
old host's car after a migration.
**Fix:** the host's respawn REQUEST 0x451570 is refused in a co-op scene / scenario while its car is the adopted mission player
object (its first call, "network game?", answers 0; the mission's loss reloads the scene for everybody ~7-10 s later); and
`I76_COOP_SCRIPT_GUARD`: those five functions return 0 (0x468440: a zeroed vector) for a NULL object, logged per skip. (A first
version returned the old car from 0x451180; that left the tick's pose list on a freed object, Strlkup `wst_swap_in`, 2 crashes.)

## 8. Sky mirror vs coop-ai (main's message; the RC twin's own log)

With `I76_SKY_MIRROR=0` the mirror calls 0x401cfe / 0x401fde, and with the tick the ai_FrameTick call 0x403ddf, are wrapped before
apply_coop_ai, whose stock-bytes check refused them: the twin's log, 144 of 145 boots "ai_FrameTick call NOT repointed", 33
"25/27". **Fix:** `st_call_site` chains them; the stubs call what the site called. ALL preset, n10: `27/27`, `ai_FrameTick call
repointed (chained)`, `sky-mirror: ON (2/2 mirror call sites)` on host and joiner.
