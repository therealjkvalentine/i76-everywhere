## 1. How teaming works (stock game, read from the code)

| question | answer | where |
|---|---|---|
| who turns teams on | the host, with the **TEAM PLAY** box on the HOST form (form button 16 toggles flag `0x8000` of the form's session block). It travels in the session's setup word as bit 3, and every machine copies it to exe `0x541010` | shell `0x10028610` case 0x10, exe `0x452db0` |
| who picks a team, and from what list | **every human picks his own team** in a popup that opens after BROADCAST GAME (host) or JOIN GAME (joiner) when the session has TEAM PLAY. The popup has **8 rows**, top to bottom: Grey Hounds, Puce Panthers, Crimson Kings, Yellow Jackets, Aqua Marines, Mauve Mayhem, Mr. Brown's Clowns, **Men In Black**, then OK / Cancel. The first row is selected by default; Cancel aborts the host / join | shell `0x1000bdd0` (rows 0..7 hit-tested, 8 = OK, 9 = Cancel; rows at `0x100441b0`), names `0x1004b920` |
| the "last team on the list" | **Men In Black, team index 7.** The exe's own name table has 12 names (Red Dawn, Gang Green, Purple Reign, Black Plague after it), but the popup shows 8, the team is kept in 3 bits, and the standings loop over 8 | exe `0x4c2130`, `0x452a20` (loop to `0x4c2150`) |
| where a human's team lives | the pick goes into the player's ANet player data (byte 1 = team + 1); every machine copies it into that player's slot flags (`0x541070 + k*0x48 + 0x1c`, bits 16..18) and from there into his car's object flags (`+0x10`, bits 16..18) when the car spawns | shell `0x10015af0` / `0x10015b90`, exe `0x4541b0`, `0x451180` |
| **team play off** | every machine puts **itself on team 0 and every other human on team 1** (`(id != me) << 16`). So without team play "the other team" is simply "everyone else" | exe `0x4541b0` |
| what teams change in play | target lock skips cars of your own team (`0x461040`); AI hold fire when a same-team car is in the line of fire (ai.md C19); radar / label colour by team (`0x45bc20`, table `0x54a46c`) | |
| kills and scores | killing a car of **another** team scores (stock formula `0x454ff0`); killing a **teammate scores nothing** and the victim gets no death (`0x464890` / `0x46ce30` credit only if the teams differ, `0x463a80` skips the death); being killed with no killer (suicide, wreck, AI) costs 500 and a death (`0x455130`) | |
| standings and the win | with TEAM PLAY the standings and the MAX SCORE test sum the scores per team ("Team %s has reached the maximum score!") | `0x452a20`, `0x454ea0` -> `0x452930` |
| friendly fire | not stopped, as far as the code was read: `entity_DamageAndKill` has one team test, at the death credit, none before the damage; the AI's fire hold is the only other guard. Not measured | `0x463a80` |

## 2. The owner's rule, as built (`b462a08`, `8315de9`)

| piece | behaviour |
|---|---|
| AI team | with TEAM PLAY on, every AI car starts on **Men In Black (7)**, pool and in-play alike (the arena file already says 7). A human who picks Men In Black plays **on the AI's side** |
| who the AI attack | `I76_AIMELEE_TEAMS` now defaults to **auto**: the team rule is on exactly when the session has TEAM PLAY. With the rule an AI attacks the nearest live car of another team: humans, and AI cars of another team (so an AI standing in for a human fights like him). `0` = never (AI attack every human), `1` = always. Team play off: nothing changes |
| a human drops | his team is noted (player id and team from the slot table); the AI that the fill brings back for him **moves to his team** (`mpf_move_team`, the same list move the co-op copies use) and fights for it |
| a human joins | the car parked for him is, in order: a live AI of **his** team, a wreck of his team, a live Men In Black AI, a Men In Black wreck, any other (farthest from every human within a class). So a team that got an AI stand-in gets the human back in its place, and team sizes stay even. A parked car goes back to Men In Black. A leave and a join of the same team at the same moment cancel |
| every screen agrees | the host sends each AI car's team in byte 3 of its 'AT' record (`0x80 | team`; the byte was 0, older proxies ignore it); a joiner moves its copy to that team list. Only in `ai_*.lvl` with the rule on: co-op scenes send 0 as before |
| own-team kill | a human's kill of an AI of his own team shows "<AI> was destroyed by <player>" but **scores nothing** (the stock team-kill rule); 'AK' byte 3 carries it |
| a joiner's team, read safely | measured: a joiner's slot first shows flags `0x1` with team 0, then `0x2` + his real team (`0x4541b0` sets bit 1 only once it read the team from the player data). The fill acts on teams only once every slot has bit 1 (at most 15 s) and the id / team set has held 3 s (`8315de9`; before it, 1 of 3 sessions gave B's stand-in team 0) |
| wave reload | the reload brings every car back on Men In Black; the host keeps a per-team count of AI stand-ins (same arena, same session) and moves that many back to their teams once the fill has settled |
| switches | `I76_AIMELEE_TEAMS` (auto / 0 / 1), `I76_AIMELEE_FILL_TEAM` (default 1; `0` = every AI stays on Men In Black). Rows in `music-fix/README.md` |
| logs | `aimelee:` status line: per AI `t<team>` and its target (slot, or 100 + label of an AI car), `teamplay` / `rule`, every human `id:t<slot team>/<car team>`; `mpfill:` line: `/t<team>` per car, the per-team ledger and queues; event lines `mpfill: teams - ...` |

Not changed: the scoreboard has no AI rows, so a team's sum is its humans' scores (AI kills by humans count for the
human's team through 'AK'); the death penalty for being killed by an AI (stock -500).
