# Stock force feedback across Alt-Tab and level restarts (2026-10-24)

Owner report, 2026-10-24 20:50, `Games\I76\release-candidate\PLAY.bat` (proxy 53944fb2), stock FFB on the T300, no interposer:
"after restarting the level and tabbing away lost FFB, now it's back as I'm typing? then lost it again, then came back, then had
a crash." The RC's `mciproxy.log` line 79358: `CRASH 0xC0000005 at I7_SFRCE.DLL+0x2505 read 0x00000000`, edx 0, stack word
`80040205` at [esp+0x10]. Owner's requirement (relayed): FFB should stay tied to the game regardless of focus or pause; re-acquire
only as the fallback for a device that was really lost.

## 1. The sequence (static, I7_SFRCE.DLL md5 687cc35b, i76.exe 6319abf7)

`I7_SFRCE.DLL` is the SideWinder SDK helper (FFB-DESIGN-LIBRARY.md correction L084). Exports: InitSystem +0x1b30,
ExitSystem +0x1d00, SIM_Effect +0x36d0. Disassembly helper: lab `tools\ffbfocus\sfdis.py`, `xref.py`.

1. **Boot** (`0x402f93` -> `0x445a60` -> InitSystem): device open +0x3850 = DirectInputCreateA(0x500), EnumDevices(joystick,
   FORCEFEEDBACK), CreateDevice, QI IDirectInputDevice2A, SetDataFormat(c_dfDIJoystick), **SetCooperativeLevel(hwnd = [+0x138c4],
   9 = DISCL_EXCLUSIVE | DISCL_BACKGROUND)**, autocenter off, **Acquire - the only Acquire the DLL ever calls.** ([+0x138c4] is
   written once, by DllMain, with the DLL's own HINSTANCE, not a window: the DLL hands DirectInput its module handle as the
   cooperative window.) The cooperative level the owner asked for (background + exclusive) is already what the stock driver
   sets; nothing to change there.
2. **Focus loss**: WndProc WM_ACTIVATEAPP 0 (`0x404aba`): ShowWindow(SW_MINIMIZE), active flag [0x504c1c] = 0, and in a mission
   (`[0x4fe534] == 1`) **`call 0x445b60` at 0x404afb = ffb_ForcesOff**: block+8 = 0, ffb_PlayEffect -> SIM_Effect. The DLL
   (+0x373a) sees forces off: `SendForceFeedbackCommand(DISFFC_STOPALL)` and **Release of the six weapon-slot effects**
   (+0x13998..+0x139ac, their caches zeroed). The other effects (engine +0x13994 / +0x13990 / +0x1398c, +0x139b0..+0x139d8) are
   stopped and kept, and nothing restarts them until the exe asks for that effect again. **This is the "lost FFB after tabbing
   away", stock behaviour by design, with or without any DirectInput problem.**
3. **Focus return**: WM_ACTIVATEAPP 1 (`0x404aa7`) = ffb_ForcesOn: the DLL runs its handlers again. Weapon-slot effects are
   re-created lazily on the next shot by the template creator (+0x2336 `call ecx` -> CreateEffect). "Came back" = the next event
   that starts an effect.
4. **The crash** (+0x2200 function, the weapon slots): when the creator fails, the slot stays NULL, but the code goes on; when the
   shot's parameter differs from the cached one (+0x24c7), it calls Stop ([edx+0x20], +0x24cf) or GetParameters ([edx+0x14],
   **the faulting `mov eax,[edx]` at +0x2505**) on the NULL slot. The failure code in the crash record's stack, [esp+0x10] =
   the creator's HRESULT (+0x2353) = **0x80040205 DIERR_NOTEXCLUSIVEACQUIRED**: at that moment the device was not
   exclusively acquired, and the DLL never re-acquires.

## 2. The fix (proxy, `music-fix/ffbfocus.c`, Gold only, only for that DLL build)

`I76_FFB_REACQUIRE` (default **1**; `=0` turns all of it off), `I76_FFB_KEEP_ON_BLUR` (default 1 under it), `I76_FFB_TRACE`
(default 0: every DirectInput call of the DLL logged). Rides on the existing 5-byte hook of ffb_PlayEffect `0x446110` (the
I76_FFB_GUARD site; the guard's device test is still only on with I76_FFB_GUARD / the LAN preset).

1. **Keep-on-blur** (the owner's requirement): `call 0x445b60` at 0x404afb -> `call ffr_on_blur` (logs only). Focus loss no longer turns the forces off,
   so the DLL neither STOPALLs nor releases its effects: the device (already background + exclusive) and its effects stay with the
   game while it is minimised or paused; on return nothing has to be re-created. ForcesOn on activation finds them on.
2. **NULL-slot guard**: +0x24b2 (`mov eax,[esp+0x38]; cmp [eax+8],ebp; je +0x2547`, 13 bytes) -> jmp to a stub that tests the
   slot (edx) first and takes the function's own +0x2547 path when it is NULL (that path NULL-checks before SetParameters and
   Start). A shot with no effect object loses its kick; the game does not die. Counted and logged.
3. **Re-acquire fallback**: when the game is active, forces on and the game's own effects enabled (`[0x52bbe4] != 0`, i.e. not the
   custom interposer's session), Acquire on every activation, every 120 FFB ticks, and right after any CreateEffect /
   SetParameters / Start / Download of the DLL failing with NOTEXCLUSIVEACQUIRED / NOTACQUIRED / INPUTLOST (that call is retried
   once). Acquire returning DI_OK (it had been lost) is logged as RE-ACQUIRED and the infinite effects that were playing are
   started again.
4. **Restart** (fallback for keep-on-blur off or a lost device): infinite-duration effects stopped by a STOPALL while the game was
   inactive, or by a lost acquisition, get Start(1, 0) on the next activation.

Observation: the DLL's IDirectInputDevice2A (Acquire, Unacquire, CreateEffect, GetForceFeedbackState, SendForceFeedbackCommand)
and IDirectInputEffect (Release, GetParameters, SetParameters, Start, Stop, Download) vtable entries in dinput.dll are pointed at
counting wrappers. Every activation / deactivation line carries GetForceFeedbackState and the number of effects the device
reports playing (GetEffectStatus).

Interposer / launcher cases: PLAY-STABLE and PLAY-VANILLA run this proxy with no I76_FFB_* key, so the default (on) applies; a
launch with the interposer (`[0x52bbe4]` zeroed by Tel-SetEngineFfb) gets the guard and keep-on-blur but never an Acquire from us.

- **"Lost FFB after tabbing away"** is the stock path on every focus loss (n 17 of 17): the infinite effects (engine and road, started
  once with Start(1, 0)) are stopped by STOPALL and not started again until the exe asks for that effect anew (a gear change, a
  weapon, a collision: "it came back"). With keep-on-blur they play straight through the time in the background (n 12 of 12) and the
  device stays acquired (GetForceFeedbackState 0 at every loss and return).
- **Weapon effects on the T300**: CreateEffect of the DLL's template at +0x3f29 (effect GUID e84cd1c7-81fa-11d0-94ab-0080c74c7e95, not
  a standard DirectInput type) fails with 0x80040154 (class not registered) on every shot (143 / 197 / 234 per run): that slot never
  has an effect object on this wheel, focus or not. It is the same NULL slot as the owner's crash; in these runs the parameter-change
  condition for the fault was not met (0 counted skips under the refined counter), so the guard is shown by its read-back and its code
  path, not by a caught fault.
- **The owner's 0x80040205 (NOTEXCLUSIVEACQUIRED) was not reproduced**: in 31 focus cycles and 9 Replays the device never lost its
  exclusive acquisition (no RE-ACQUIRED line, no failed Acquire). The re-acquire stays as the fallback; what took the device from the
  owner's game is open (another program taking the T300 exclusively for a moment is the candidate: an FFB tool, the interposer, a
  second game).
- **The before-run's exit crash was ours**: ExitSystem released the device during the game's exit, then a WM_ACTIVATEAPP 0 reached
  ffb_PlayEffect and the observe-only tick called GetForceFeedbackState on the freed object (ntdll write 0x14, stack 0x446115 /
  0x404b00). Fixed in c975f59: the tick follows the DLL's device pointer and init flag and stands down. With keep-on-blur on (the
  default) WM_ACTIVATEAPP 0 does not reach ffb_PlayEffect at all.
- **Regression, gate row 9 (T05 route) on ffb-twin with 5d6f9828**: PASS, hill leg median 108.6 fps over 2611 m (threshold 108);
  rows 1 / S / D PASS; 0 crash records. Earlier RC gates read 118.2 / 119.5 on rc-twin, hence the same-conditions A/B below.
