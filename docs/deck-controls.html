Interstate '76 on the Deck: what every control does

The layout this project installs on the Deck ("Option 1 v8.1"). Driving is analog on the left stick, the triggers fire, and the rarely used keys sit in a menu on the left trackpad. Inputs marked in yellow behave differently from what you might expect.

Watch for these

Y does two things.

A single tap goes to the chase camera. A quick double-tap goes back into the cockpit, and another double-tap there switches between the full dashboard and the open view with small gauges. Tap slowly and you get the chase camera twice.

A is both Enter and a mouse click.

It presses Enter and clicks wherever the pointer is. In menus, park the pointer somewhere harmless if you only mean Enter. In a mission a left click fires hardpoint 1, so A does too.

Both sticks click.

Pressing the left stick in is special 1, which on most cars is the nitrous. Pressing the right stick in turns your view toward the current target. Both are easy to hit by accident in a hard turn.

The right stick depends on the view.

Inside the car it glances and springs back when you let go. Outside the car it swings the camera around the car, and the camera stays where you leave it.

The trackpad menu needs a light touch.

Rest your thumb on the left trackpad to bring up the 13-slice menu, slide to a slice, then press. The "Combat camera" slice switches to the camera behind the car; the dashboard itself is the Y double-tap.

R5 fires two hardpoints.

The lower right grip fires hardpoints 4 and 5 at the same time, for as long as you hold it. Hardpoint 3 is on the upper right grip, R4. Dropping a target has no button in this layout.

A second library entry, "Interstate 76 (pad layer)", plays with the Windows and Mac pad controls instead (Y cycles the views, LB is a shift layer); the grips and trackpads work the same in both. The layout is a Steam Input template; pick it under the game's controller settings, Templates, "Interstate 76 - Option 1 v8.1 (all-in-one wheel)". Steam + X opens the on-screen keyboard for anything not listed here.
